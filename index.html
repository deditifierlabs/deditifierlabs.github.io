Deditifier Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Deditifier Labs

Deditifier is a PDF reader and editor that works entirely on your PC.

What Deditifier collects

Nothing. Deditifier has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet itself.

Your documents

Deditifier opens, shows and changes only the files you choose. They are processed on your PC and are never uploaded, copied elsewhere or shared with anyone, including us.

Your signature

If you tick "Remember", the signature you draw, type or upload in Fill & Sign is saved on your PC only, encrypted with Windows so that only your Windows account can read it. You can remove it at any time: untick "Remember" the next time you add a signature. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version of Deditifier also deletes it.

Purchases

Deditifier Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Payment and licence details are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Deditifier only asks the Store whether this copy has Pro and never sees your payment details.

Error logs

If something goes wrong, Deditifier writes a short technical log on your PC. It never contains your documents' content, stays on your PC and is never sent anywhere.

Children

Deditifier does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
